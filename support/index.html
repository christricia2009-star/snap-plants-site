FAQ

Common questions

How accurate is plant identification?
Identification is assistive and can be wrong. Accuracy depends on photo quality, lighting, plant uniqueness, and category selection. Results are educational — always verify important decisions (especially toxicity or edibility) with trusted sources or a professional. The app also surfaces helpful lookup links for further reading.
Do I need Sign in with Apple?
You can use many features without signing in. Sign in with Apple unlocks cloud backup and restore of your plant collection, and features that involve other users — such as posting trades and in-app chat.
How do plant trades work?
Trades are peer-to-peer. Post a trade, browse the trade feed, open trade detail, and chat in-app. You can swipe right on a collection plant to pre-fill a trade. Set up contact helpers (username / Facebook) so others know how to reach you. Snap Plants does not process payments or ship plants for you.
Does cloud backup require sign-in?
Yes. Cloud backup and restore of your plant collection is available while signed in. You can also export your collection (CSV or similar) for a local portable copy when the app supports it.
What happens to my plant photos?
The camera is used for plant identification and collection photos. We don’t sell your photos or use them for advertising. Optional trade contact info is provided by you. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Indoor vs outdoor categories — does it matter?
Choosing a category on the scan flow (for example indoor vs outdoor plant types) helps produce more relevant identification results. If you’re unsure, pick the closest match — or add the plant manually and edit details later.
When will Snap Plants be on the App Store?
Snap Plants is coming to the App Store for iOS. For feedback or early access, email admin@snapcollectibles.com. We’ll add an App Store or TestFlight link on the homepage when available.
Can I trust toxicity or safety notes?
Any care or safety context in the app is educational only and may be incomplete or incorrect. Snap Plants does not guarantee medical, veterinary, or toxicity advice. Always verify with a veterinarian, poison control, or other qualified professional for real-world safety decisions.
