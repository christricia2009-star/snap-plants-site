How it works

Snap → Identify → Track → Trade

Four simple steps from a photo to a living collection — and trades when you’re ready.

Step 01

Snap & pick a category

Open the camera scan, choose a category (indoor, outdoor, and more), then take a clear photo of your plant.

  • Category selection on the scan flow
  • Indoor and outdoor plant types
  • Or add a plant manually anytime
Step 02

See identification results

Get assistive plant diagnosis and identification results — plus helpful lookup links to plant databases and references when you want a second look.

  • Plant ID / diagnosis results
  • Helpful lookup & reference links
  • Assistive only — not expert botanical advice
Step 03

Track your collection

Save plants to My Plants, browse My Shelf as a photo shelf, add notes and location tags, and keep a wishlist for plants you want next.

  • My Plants list & plant detail
  • My Shelf photo display
  • Wishlist, notes & location tags
Step 04

Trade with other plant parents

Post plant trades, browse the trade feed, open trade details, and chat in-app. Swipe right on a collection plant to pre-fill a trade.

  • Trade posts, feed & detail
  • In-app chat between traders
  • Contact setup (username / Facebook helpers)

FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers about ID, trades, backup, and privacy.

How accurate is plant identification?
Identification is assistive and can be wrong. Photo quality, lighting, and similar-looking species all affect results. Use the app’s lookup links, and always verify important decisions (especially anything related to edibility or toxicity) with trusted sources or a professional.
Do I need to Sign in with Apple?
You can explore much of the app without an account. Sign in with Apple unlocks account-backed features such as cloud backup and restore of your plant collection, and features that involve other users (like trades).
How do trades work?
Trades are peer-to-peer. You can post a plant trade, browse the trade feed, open trade detail, and chat in-app. Set up contact helpers (username / Facebook) so others know how to reach you. Snap Plants does not handle payments or shipping for you.
Does cloud backup require sign-in?
Yes. Cloud backup and restore of your plant collection is available while you’re signed in. You can also export your collection (CSV or similar) for a local copy when the app supports it.
Indoor vs outdoor categories — does it matter?
Yes — choosing a category on the scan flow (for example indoor vs outdoor types) helps the app give more relevant identification results. If you’re unsure, pick the closest match or add the plant manually later.
What about my camera and privacy?
The camera is used for plant identification and collection photos. Optional trade contact info (like username or Facebook helpers) is provided by you. We don’t sell your photos. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
When will Snap Plants be on the App Store?
Snap Plants is coming to the App Store for iOS. For early access or feedback, email admin@snapcollectibles.com. We’ll post an App Store link here when it’s available.
