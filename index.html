Free and Pro

What stays free, and what Pro adds.

Free

Collector basics

  • Unlimited collection, catalog browsing, and browsing trades and events.
  • 25 plant identifications and care lookups per calendar month.
  • 1 trade listing.
  • Interest on 1 other listing.
  • 20 messages on each of those two threads.
Snap Plants Pro

$14.99 per year or $1.99 per month

  • Billed by Apple.
  • Unlimited identification and lookups.
  • Unlimited listings, interests, and chat.
  • Multi-model identify.
  • Posting plant events plus event chat.

Trades and identification

Notes, not a checkout.

Dollar and shipping fields on a trade are notes. The app does not take payment for plants.

Identification is educational and is not professional botanical advice. Confirm identity and toxicity before ingesting a plant or placing it near pets.
