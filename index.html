How it works · iOS

Four steps from snap
to thriving green

A guided walkthrough of the iOS Snap Plants experience.

Step 01

Snap & choose a category

Open Snap, pick a type — houseplants, succulents, herbs — or tap Not Sure? Point your camera.

  • Houseplants, succulents & cacti
  • Outdoor garden, trees & shrubs
  • Herbs, vegetables, wildflowers
Step 02

Instant plant reveal

Meet your plant by name with a clear confidence score and a care snapshot at a glance.

  • Common + botanical name
  • Confidence percentage
  • Light, water & humidity snapshot
Step 03

Care that goes deeper

Soil, pests, symptoms, toxicity notes, and trusted resources — all in one calm diagnosis view.

  • Symptoms & care advice
  • Pet & human toxicity notes
  • PlantNet, RHS, Wikipedia & more
Step 04

Save & track your collection

Save plants to My Plants, revisit diagnosis history, and keep lists organized.

  • My Plants with search & tabs
  • Diagnosis history over time
  • Export & import your collection

How it works · Android

From permission
to plant companion

The Android testing build — Snap, AI Botanist, Encyclopedia, and your collection.

Step 01

Grant camera access

Android asks for camera permission so you can snap plants. One tap to allow — then you’re ready to identify.

  • In-app permission prompt
  • System camera dialog
  • Free daily IDs shown in the header
Step 02

Snap to identify

Open the live camera, frame your plant, and tap the shutter. Instant identification starts here.

  • Live camera viewfinder
  • One-tap capture button
  • Bottom nav: Snap · Plants · AI · more
Step 03

Ask the AI Botanist

Chat about care, toxicity, pests, or ID tips — or browse the Plant Encyclopedia with toxicity alerts.

  • Conversational plant help
  • Encyclopedia with pet toxicity flags
  • Search by name or family
Step 04

Build your collection

Save plants to My Plants and manage the experience in Settings — designed for 1:1 feature parity with iOS.

  • My Plants collection
  • Care guides & toxic alerts
  • Settings & about the build
