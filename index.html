How it works · iOS

Four steps from snap
to thriving green

A guided walkthrough of the iOS Snap Plants experience.

Step 01

Snap & choose a category

Open Snap, pick a type — houseplants, succulents, herbs — or tap Not Sure? Point your camera.

  • Houseplants, succulents & cacti
  • Outdoor garden, trees & shrubs
  • Herbs, vegetables, wildflowers
Step 02

Instant plant reveal

Meet your plant by name with a clear confidence score and a care snapshot at a glance.

  • Common + botanical name
  • Confidence percentage
  • Light, water & humidity snapshot
Step 03

Care that goes deeper

Soil, pests, symptoms, toxicity notes, and trusted resources — all in one calm diagnosis view.

  • Symptoms & care advice
  • Pet & human toxicity notes
  • PlantNet, RHS, Wikipedia & more
Step 04

Save & track your collection

Save plants to My Plants, revisit diagnosis history, and keep lists organized.

  • My Plants with search & tabs
  • Diagnosis history over time
  • Export & import your collection

How it works · Android

From scan
to your collection

The Android testing build — Scan modes, identify categories, collection, wishlist, and selling.

Step 01

Choose a scan mode

Open Scan — free IDs left today are clear at the top. Pick how you want to help your plant.

  • Identify Plant
  • Diagnose Issue
  • Toxic Check & Weed ID
Step 02

Pick what you’re snapping

Identify Plant opens category tiles so the AI knows whether you’re looking at a leaf, flower, houseplant, or more.

  • Any Plant, Flower, Leaf
  • Tree / Shrub, Succulent, Houseplant
  • Herb, Vine / Climber
Step 03

Save to your collection

Collection holds your plants with CSV import/export and swipe actions — left to delete, right to wishlist.

  • Import / Export CSV
  • Swipe gestures
  • Empty state that guides the next snap
Step 04

Wishlist, selling & settings

Organize wishlist and selling lists, then manage your OpenRouter key, AI Botanist, and encyclopedia from Settings.

  • Wishlist & Selling lists
  • API key validate & save
  • AI Botanist + Encyclopedia shortcuts
