How it works

Four steps from snap
to thriving green

A guided walkthrough of the Snap Plants experience — the same calm flow you’ll feel in the app.

Step 01

Snap & choose a category

Open Snap, pick a type — houseplants, succulents, herbs, wildflowers — or tap Not Sure? Point your camera. That’s the whole setup.

  • Houseplants, succulents & cacti
  • Outdoor garden, trees & shrubs
  • Herbs, vegetables, wildflowers
Step 02

Instant plant reveal

Meet your plant by name — common and scientific — with a clear confidence score and a care snapshot at a glance.

  • Common + botanical name
  • Confidence percentage
  • Light, water & humidity snapshot
Step 03

Care that goes deeper

Beyond the basics: soil, pests, symptoms, toxicity notes, and trusted resources — all in one calm diagnosis view.

  • Symptoms & care advice
  • Pet & human toxicity notes
  • PlantNet, RHS, Wikipedia & more
Step 04

Save & track your collection

Save plants to My Plants, revisit diagnosis history, and keep wishlist lists organized. Your green family, all in one place.

  • My Plants with search & tabs
  • Diagnosis history over time
  • Export & import your collection
