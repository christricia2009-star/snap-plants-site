Snap Plants Pro

Snap Plants Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription billed by Apple. Monthly is $1.99 per month. Annual is $14.99 per year. The price shown on the App Store at purchase is the price charged.

Pro unlocks unlimited plant identification and lookups, unlimited trade listings, interests, and chat, multi-model identify, and posting plant events. Free includes 25 identifications and lookups each calendar month, 1 trade listing, interest on 1 listing, and 20 messages on each of those threads. The collection, catalog, and browsing trades and events stay free.

Payment is charged to the Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Trades and identification

Plant trades are between collectors. Dollar and shipping fields are notes. Snap Plants does not process those payments. Identification is educational and is not a substitute for professional botanical advice.

Community rules

These rules apply to posts, chat, and events.

  • Be respectful. No harassment, threats, hate, or sexual content.
  • No scams, impersonation, gift-card demands, or crypto payment requests.
  • List only plants you may legally possess and ship. No illegal or controlled substances.
  • Do not post secrets, street addresses, or phone numbers. Friends-only is still stored in iCloud.
  • Use Report and Block in the app. Email admin@snapcollectibles.com if a person needs to review it.
  • We may remove posts and accounts that break these rules.
