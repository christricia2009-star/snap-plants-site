Privacy / ownership

Your shelf is yours.

Snap Plants is a collector app for plants. The camera exists to photograph plants. Account features exist so the collection can follow you. Last updated August 19, 2026.

Camera and photos

Photos are used for plant identification, diagnosis, and the collection journal. They support ID and journaling — not unrelated uses. A photo cannot replace a botanist. Identifications are estimates for learning and reference, not professional botanical, medical, or veterinary advice.

  • Images may be processed by services that power identification solely to return results.
  • We do not use your plant photos for advertising, and we do not sell your photos.
  • You can deny camera or photo access in system settings; some features will not work without them.

Sign-in

iOS uses Sign in with Apple. Android uses Sign in with Google. You can scan and journal without an account. Cloud backup, restore, and trades require sign-in.

  • Collection data you save (photos, notes, location tags, wishlist, selling flags) stays in the app unless you back it up or export it.
  • Backup and restore are available while signed in.
  • You can export a CSV where the app supports it.

Trades

Trades are peer-to-peer between plant parents. Snap Plants does not insure, ship, or process payments. Contact details such as a username or optional Facebook helpers are information you choose to provide so other users can reach you.

Optional AI key

If you add an OpenRouter API key in Settings, it is stored on-device so identification and optional chat features can use the model you pick. It is not a newsletter signup. Identification remains educational.

What this marketing site collects

This site is static. The beta form sends App name, phone OS, and email to admin@snapcollectibles.com so we can issue a tester invite. No analytics SDK is bundled.

If you request the Android beta, the site shows the Google Play internal test URL on screen. You must copy and keep it. Google Play does not auto-send emails to testers. The URL becomes active once your email is added to the tester list — please allow up to a few hours.

Children

Snap Plants is not directed at children under 13.
