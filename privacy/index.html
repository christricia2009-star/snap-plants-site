Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026 · Last updated: August 7, 2026

Snap Plants (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Snap Plants mobile application and related websites (together, the “Service”). We designed this policy to be clear and transparent — so you know what happens with your photos, plant data, and account details.

1. Who we are

Snap Plants is an iOS app for identifying plants, tracking a personal plant collection, and optionally trading plants with other users. For privacy questions, contact us at admin@snapcollectibles.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process the following categories of information:

Information you provide

  • Account information — when you Sign in with Apple, we receive the identity information Apple provides (such as a user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name or email).
  • Plant collection data — plant names, photos, notes, location tags, wishlist items, and related metadata you save in the app.
  • Trade and contact details — trade posts, in-app messages, and optional contact helpers you provide (such as a username or Facebook-related helpers) so other users can reach you for peer-to-peer trades.
  • Support messages — content you send when contacting us for help.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information — device type, OS version, app version, language, and basic performance or crash diagnostics (if enabled).
  • Usage data — approximate feature usage (for example, that identification or trades were used), not the full content of every screen you view, unless needed for diagnostics you opt into.

3. Camera and photo usage

Snap Plants needs camera and/or photo library access for plant identification and collection photos. Here’s how we treat that:

  • Photos you take or select are used to generate plant identification and care context, and to display plants in your collection and My Shelf views.
  • Images may be processed by services that power identification solely to return identification and related results.
  • We do not use your plant photos for advertising, and we do not sell your photos.
  • You can deny camera or photo access in system settings; some features will not work without them.
  • Photos saved to your collection remain under your control and can be removed by deleting the entry or clearing app data.

4. Plant identification data

When you identify a plant, the Service may process the image and any category you select (for example indoor vs outdoor types) to return assistive identification results, related care context when available, and helpful lookup links to plant databases or references.

  • Identification is assistive and may be imperfect or incorrect. Results are educational, not professional botanical, medical, or veterinary advice.
  • If a third-party service processes the image for identification, that provider may temporarily process the content according to their terms and privacy practices.
  • You may delete individual plants or your collection where the app allows. Export (CSV or similar) may also be available for a local copy.

5. Accounts, cloud backup, and trades

  • Sign in with Apple — used to authenticate your account for features such as cloud backup and restore of your plant collection, and social features like trades.
  • Cloud backup / restore — while signed in, your plant collection may be backed up and restored so you can recover it across devices. Backup requires sign-in.
  • Trades — trade posts, trade details, and in-app chat are shared with the other party as needed to facilitate peer-to-peer plant trading. Contact helpers you enter are user-provided and may be visible to other users as you configure them.

6. Analytics and diagnostics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics or crash reporting to understand stability and improve the product (for example, crash logs and aggregated feature usage). We avoid collecting plant photos into analytics tools unless strictly necessary for debugging a problem you report. You can limit certain tracking through your device settings where available.

7. Third-party services

We may use trusted third parties to operate the Service, such as:

  • Sign in with Apple (account authentication)
  • Services that power plant identification
  • Infrastructure that supports accounts, cloud backup, trades, and messaging
  • Email or support tools
  • Analytics or crash reporting vendors

These providers process data only as needed to provide their services to us and are expected to protect it appropriately. External lookup links inside the app open third-party plant databases or reference sites governed by their own policies.

8. How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve plant identification, collection, and trade features
  • Save, back up (when signed in), and display your plant collection
  • Enable peer-to-peer trades and in-app chat between users
  • Respond to support requests
  • Send product updates if you opt in
  • Protect security, prevent abuse, and comply with law

9. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share plant photos or collection data with advertisers. We may share information only: with service providers under contract; with other users when you participate in trades or share contact helpers; if required by law or legal process; to protect rights, safety, or security; or with your direction (for example, when you export your collection).

10. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service and for legitimate business or legal purposes. Collection data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Children’s privacy

Snap Plants is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact admin@snapcollectibles.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you
  • Export your plant collection (where the app provides CSV or similar export)
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing (such as marketing emails)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise these rights, email admin@snapcollectibles.com. You can also manage camera/photo permissions in your device settings and delete app data by uninstalling the app.

13. International users

If you use Snap Plants from outside the country where our systems or providers operate, your information may be processed in other countries that may have different data protection laws. We take steps designed to ensure appropriate safeguards where required.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes may be highlighted in the app or on our website. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

15. Contact us
